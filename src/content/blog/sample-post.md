This is a **sample post** used to check the blog design. Because it is marked as a draft, it appears on preview links but is never published on www.divyataa.in. Real posts replace it.

## A section heading

Posts are written in plain text with a few simple marks. A blank line starts a new paragraph. Two stars make text **bold**, and a [link to the Contact page](/contact) looks like this.

### A smaller heading

- A bulleted list item
- Another item, with a little more text so it wraps onto a second line on a phone screen
- A third item

1. A numbered step
2. A second step
3. A third step
